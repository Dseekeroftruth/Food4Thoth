1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Arp Synth Sequencer ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our browser-based arpeggiator synthesizer and fractal visualizer application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This App is designed to work entirely within your browser or device without transmitting any personal data to any server.

Plain language: We don't know who you are, and we don't try to find out. The App runs on your device only.

2 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • Device identifiers (UDID, IDFA, IDFV, Android Advertising ID, etc.)
  • IP addresses or location data
  • Microphone recordings or audio input
  • Usage statistics, session duration, or interaction logs
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent off-device
  • Payment or financial information
  • Cookies or persistent identifiers of any kind

3 How the App Works

Arp Synth Sequencer is a music creation and visualization tool. All processing occurs entirely on your local device:

  • Audio synthesis is generated locally using the Web Audio API (via Tone.js). No audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • Visual animations are rendered in your browser's canvas element using JavaScript. No images are captured or sent.
  • User settings (BPM, chord selection, effect parameters) exist only in memory during your session and are not saved or transmitted anywhere.
  • The App does not require a user account, login, or any form of registration.

4 Permissions

The App may request the following browser or device permissions:

  • Audio Output: Required to play synthesized music through your device's speakers or headphones. The App does not access your microphone and does not record audio input.

No other permissions — such as camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, or notifications — are requested or required.

5 Third-Party Services

The App uses the following open-source library, loaded from a public CDN:

  • Tone.js — a JavaScript audio framework served from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Loading this resource causes a standard HTTPS request to jsDelivr's servers, which may log your IP address as part of normal CDN operation. Please refer to jsDelivr's privacy policy at jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy for details.

We do not integrate any advertising networks, analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.), social media SDKs, or crash reporting services.

6 Children's Privacy

Arp Synth Sequencer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain EU member states under GDPR).

Because the App does not collect any data whatsoever, it presents no special risk to younger users. However, we encourage parents and guardians to supervise their children's online activities.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at the address below and we will take appropriate steps to delete such information.

7 Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal data at risk on our end. The App operates entirely within your browser environment.

We still encourage you to keep your device, browser, and operating system up to date to benefit from the latest security patches provided by your device manufacturer and browser vendor.

8 Data Retention

We retain no data about you because we collect no data about you. Any preferences or settings you configure within the App exist only in your device's active memory and are discarded when you close the App or browser tab.

9 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable privacy law, including:

  • GDPR (EU/EEA/UK): Right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): Right to know, delete, opt-out of sale, and non-discrimination
  • PIPEDA (Canada): Right to access and correct personal information

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. If you have questions or concerns, please contact us using the information below.
