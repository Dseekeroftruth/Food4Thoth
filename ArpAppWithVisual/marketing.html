Getting Started

Up and running in seconds

No downloads, no sign-ups, no plugins — just open and play.

1

Open the app in any browser

Works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. No install, no account.

2

Tap "Start Audio"

Browsers require a user gesture to activate audio. One tap unlocks full sound and kicks off all three visualizers.

3

Pick a chord and pattern

Choose from 60+ chord presets or type your own. Set the arpeggio pattern (Up, Down, Random…) and step interval.

4

Shape your sound

Dial in effects, switch synth type, hit Warp for instant surprises. Use See-Through mode to immerse in the visuals.

5

Perform & enjoy

Everything updates in real time — BPM, chord, effects — without ever stopping playback.
