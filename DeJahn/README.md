# Overview

DeJahns Music Collection is an experimental, immersive multimedia experience blending sonic innovation with glitch aesthetics, parallax illusions, and a sprawling digital gallery of sound art. This page is a portal into the kaleidoscopic mind of DeJahn—a musical explorer pushing boundaries through raw emotion, surreal soundscapes, and boundary-breaking improvisation.

---

# Features
- Glitch-Effect Hero & Animations
A captivating intro with animated glitch text and ambient background color-shift illusions.
- Sticky Neumorphic Navigation
Access dozens of portals, tools, collectives, and games via an expandable, mobile-friendly nav.
- Slideshow Art Gallery
Scrollable image showcase featuring photos and performance stills.
- Video Archive
Embedded videos from YouTube and Facebook, grouped by theme (Improv, Drum Kits, HAPI Pan, Experimental Lunch Breaks, and more).
- Parallax Sections
Rich immersive sections with layered backgrounds for philosophical statements and creative expression.
- Music Integration with Community
