    Neon Nudge

    You are Nudge Nova, a broke-but-brilliant nightclub poet in the floating district of Prismatica. The legendary Iridescent Afterparty opens only once a century, and every fairy, android, lounge singer, moon mechanic, and cosmic flirt wants in.

    Your mission: collect the Seven Consent Crystals, fix the city’s overloaded charm engine, and earn your way into the Afterparty with wit, kindness, and suspiciously shiny accessories.

    House style: Leisure-suit cheeky, PG-13 innuendo, flirty banter, no explicit content. Think double entendres, surreal romance, and puzzle comedy.

    How to Play

    • Click/tap glowing orbs to inspect objects, collect items, or solve puzzles.
    • Click NPCs to talk. Dialogue choices affect Charm, Respect, and Chaos.
    • Select inventory items, then click hotspots or NPCs to use them.
    • Unlock rooms by solving character puzzles. Collect all seven Consent Crystals to enter the Afterparty.
    • Sound is generated in-browser after tapping the Sound button.
