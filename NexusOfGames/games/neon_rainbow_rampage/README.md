# NEON RAINBOW RAMPAGE 🌈

Psychedelic music-reactive side-scroller. 3 fighters, 3 levels, power-ups, and
a directional block button.

## Controls

| Action       | Keyboard     | Touch                  |
|--------------|--------------|------------------------|
| Move         | ← → / A D    | ◀ ▶                    |
| Jump         | W            | JUMP                   |
| **Block**    | **S**        | **BLOCK**              |
| Aim up       | ↑ (hold)     | AIM↑ (hold)            |
| Shoot        | SPACE        | FIRE                   |
| Music        | M            | ♪ (top-right)          |

### Block direction matters

- **Hold BLOCK alone** → forward shield. Catches contact damage and bullets
  from the direction you're facing.
- **Hold BLOCK + AIM↑** → overhead shield. Catches incoming downward bullets,
  including the descending shots from hot air balloons.

Block costs stamina (⚡ in the HUD). While blocking you can't move, jump, or
shoot. Stamina regenerates after a short delay.

### Aim up + fire

Hold AIM↑ + FIRE for 45° diagonal shots — the only way to reach high balloons.

## iPhone install

1. AirDrop or email `index.html` to your iPhone.
2. Open from Files → Open in Safari.
3. Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen for full-screen app experience.

## Characters

- **NOVA** — balanced (100 HP, magenta/cyan)
- **BLAZE** — tank (140 HP, orange, wider build, slower fire)
- **ZEN** — glass cannon (80 HP, green, triple-shot default)

## Levels

1. **NEON CITY** — 135 BPM, balanced enemy mix
2. **CYBER JUNGLE** — 155 BPM, ninja-heavy
3. **SKY ARMADA** — 172 BPM, balloon swarm (endless)

## Power-ups

- **+** Health (+35 HP)
- **S** Spread (+2 bullets fan, 15s)
- **R** Rapid fire (halved cooldown, 15s)
- **◉** Shield (full invulnerability, 10s)
- **B** Bomb (clears all on-screen enemies)
