A hyper-color music-reactive psychedelic side-scroller for iPhone touch, desktop keyboard, and wild Food4Thoth-style expansion.
Move: touch arrows, WASD, or arrow keys.
Jump: JUMP button, W, ↑, or Space.
Fire: FIRE button, F, K, or tap-hold fire.
Special: BURST button, E, L, or collect drum sigils.
Power Ups: health hearts, shield halos, rainbow speed, laser drums, double score.
Enemies: fighting ninjas, cyberpunk motorcycles, pogo cartoons, and machine-gun hot-air balloons.
