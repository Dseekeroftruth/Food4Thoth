# All the King’s Horses and All the King’s Men

*The Trump-Epstein Axis, Elite Impunity, and the Unbroken Line of Obstruction (1990s–2025)*

---

## Overview

This digital dissertation documents and analyzes the entwined social, financial, and legal histories of Donald Trump and Jeffrey Epstein, tracing the networks of complicity and protection that enabled serial abuse, normalized obstruction, and undermined the rule of law. Combining court records, investigative journalism, survivor testimony, and legislative history, it argues that elite impunity is not an aberration but a systemic feature of American power.

The project investigates the machinery of legal defense, prosecutorial deals, media suppression, and political manipulation—highlighting the roles of high-profile lawyers, conflicted DOJ officials, and “catch and kill” media tactics. It concludes by assessing the continued danger to American democracy posed by the normalization of elite criminality and institutional failure.
