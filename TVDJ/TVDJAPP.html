Welcome to TVDJ

Turn any device into a live video/audio DJ performance instrument.

1Tap any pad to load a video or audio file from your device.
2Press a loaded pad to play. Press again to stop (toggle mode).
3Long-press or right-click a pad to set loop points, volume & trigger mode.
4Use TAP to set BPM. Transport controls manage all playing pads.
5Hit the pink download button (bottom right) to save this app offline.
