What you get

Everything you need
to perform.

No bloat. No complexity. Just a clean, powerful interface built for live performance.

12 Trigger Pads
A 3x4 grid of color-coded pads lets you fire any clip instantly. Each pad pulses and glows when active for clear visual feedback on stage.
Any Format Welcome
Import MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG and more. No conversion needed. If your device plays it, TVDJ can trigger it.
Per-Pad Clip Editing
Set custom start and end points for every clip. Adjust individual pad volume. Trim your samples to the exact moment without touching the original file.
Sample Library
All your clips in one searchable place. Name them, organize them, reassign them to any pad. Your library grows with you.
Master Transport
Global play, stop, and master volume control. Take command of your entire set from a single panel at the bottom of the screen.
Fully Offline
Everything lives on your device. No account. No cloud. No subscription. No internet required on stage. Your music, your hardware.

Get started in minutes

Three steps to your
first live set.

01
Upload Your Clips
Go to the Library tab and upload any video or audio file from your iPhone. Any format works — MP4, MP3, WAV, MOV, and more.
02
Assign to Pads
Tap any empty pad on the Perform screen and choose a sample from your library. Arrange your set exactly how you want it.
03
Perform Live
Tap pads to fire samples in real time. Adjust master volume on the fly. Hold any pad to edit its clip on the spot. You are ready.
