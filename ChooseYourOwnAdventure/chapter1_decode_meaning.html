Chapter 1: Decode the Glyphs

You sit with the compass, its alien glyphs shimmering faintly. With careful focus, you begin to study their shapes and patterns. The symbols seem alive, shifting as your mind traces their meaning. Gradually, a connection forms between the glyphs and your thoughts, as if they’re responding to your curiosity.

As you decode their intricate patterns, a story emerges—a tale of travelers who once walked this path, guided by the same compass. They left behind messages hidden within the glyphs, warnings and lessons for those who would follow.

“The compass reveals many paths,” a voice seems to echo from within. “But understanding its meaning is the first step to mastering your journey.”

What do you choose to do next?

Follow the path of the ancient travelers. Continue decoding the glyphs to uncover deeper meanings. Test the compass to see how it reacts to your knowledge.
