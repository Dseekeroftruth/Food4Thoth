Chapter 3: Call for Help

The weight of the moment presses down on you, the glowing compass flickering faintly in your hand. The world around you seems to hold its breath as shadows draw closer. In the stillness, you feel a spark of connection—an ancient force ready to respond, if only you would ask.

You close your eyes and focus inward, letting your intention ripple outward like waves in a still pond. You whisper your plea for guidance, not with words but with your very essence. The air around you shifts, and a soft, melodic hum fills the clearing.

Light begins to weave through the darkness, forming the outline of a figure. It steps forward, radiant and calm, its presence both comforting and awe-inspiring. Its voice resonates within you:

“You are not alone. To ask for help is a sign of strength, not weakness. How will you use the guidance you seek?”

The figure extends its hand, offering choices that will shape your next step.

What will you do?

Accept the figure’s guidance and follow their lead. Ask the figure questions about your path. Walk forward together, sharing the journey.
