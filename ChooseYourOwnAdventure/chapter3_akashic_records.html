Chapter 3: Enter the Akashic Records

The compass glows brighter, its light shifting into cascading threads of golden energy. The forest around you dissolves, revealing an immense library stretching endlessly in every direction. The shelves are filled with glowing scrolls, each pulsating with energy. A sense of timelessness envelops you as you step forward, drawn deeper into this ethereal space.

A figure cloaked in starlight appears before you, their voice resonating in your mind:

“Welcome to the Akashic Records, the library of all knowledge and existence. Here, the story of every soul, every choice, and every moment is written. What you seek is already within you. What will you uncover?”

The figure gestures toward the shelves, and a glowing scroll floats into your hands. It pulses with warmth, inviting you to open it. The air hums with energy as you prepare to explore the records of existence.

What will you focus on?

Open the scroll to explore your past lives. Seek universal truths about existence and creation. Search for guidance on your current journey.
