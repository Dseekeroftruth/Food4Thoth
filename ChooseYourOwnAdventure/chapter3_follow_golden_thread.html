Chapter 3: Follow the Golden Thread

The golden thread glows warmly, pulsing with a rhythm that feels connected to your own heartbeat. You reach out and touch it, and in an instant, you feel yourself drawn into its flow. It weaves through the darkness, guiding you toward an unseen destination.

The journey feels endless, yet timeless. Along the way, you see flashes of memories, moments from your past and fragments of your possible future. The thread vibrates with energy, as if whispering to you:

“This is the connection between all things—your past, present, and future. Follow, and you will understand.”

The thread leads you to a radiant nexus, where countless threads converge. Each one seems to represent a choice, a path, a connection. The golden thread you followed pulses brightly, inviting you to make a decision.

What will you do at the nexus?

Choose a new path and step into the unknown. Explore the threads and understand their connections. Follow the golden thread deeper into the unknown.
