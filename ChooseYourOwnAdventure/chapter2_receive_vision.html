Chapter 2: Receive the Vision

As the shaman chants and the smoke from the sacred herbs swirls around you, your senses begin to shift. The physical world fades, replaced by a radiant tapestry of light and energy. A vision begins to form, vivid and alive, as if the universe itself is speaking to you.

Images flash before your eyes: a spiraling galaxy, a towering tree with roots reaching deep into the earth, and a golden thread weaving through both darkness and light. A deep, resonant voice echoes in your mind:

“The path is both within and beyond. What you see here is not destiny—it is potential. The choices you make will shape what comes.”

The vision grows clearer, and you must decide where to focus:

Follow the golden thread and see where it leads. Explore the spiraling galaxy and its mysteries. Uncover the secrets held in the tree’s deep roots.
