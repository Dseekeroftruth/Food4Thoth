Chapter 1: Unlock the Compass

Determined to understand its true power, you focus intently on the alien glyphs inscribed on the compass. They shimmer and shift, forming patterns that seem to pulse in harmony with your heartbeat. Slowly, you begin to trace the shapes with your fingers, feeling their energy resonate through your body.

A soft hum fills the air as the compass reacts to your intent. Suddenly, the glyphs glow brighter, and the compass vibrates with increasing intensity. A beam of golden light bursts forth, splitting the mist around you and revealing a glowing gateway.

“You have unlocked the path,” a voice whispers from within the compass. “The gateway leads to knowledge, but only those with clarity may pass.”

What will you do?

Step through the glowing gateway. Stay and analyze the glyphs further. Seek the shaman’s guidance about the gateway.
