Chapter 3: Explore the Origin of the Light

As the light envelops you, you feel a pull toward its source. It beckons, offering glimpses of something ancient, vast, and unknowable. You follow the pull, stepping deeper into the radiance. The world around you shifts, dissolving into patterns of swirling stars and glowing threads of energy.

Memories not your own flash through your mind—visions of creation, destruction, and renewal, as if the light has witnessed the birth and rebirth of countless worlds. A voice, deep and resonant, echoes through the expanse:

“I am the spark from which all things emerge. To know me is to know the essence of existence. But be warned—light reveals as much as it blinds.”

The light pulses, offering pathways to uncover its mysteries. You feel a choice before you, each path leading to a deeper understanding of the light’s origin.

Where will you go?

Trace the light to its point of creation. Understand the light’s role in the balance of existence. Merge fully with the light’s source to uncover its truth.
