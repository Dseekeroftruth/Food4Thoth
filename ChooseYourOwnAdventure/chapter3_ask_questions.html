Chapter 3: Ask Questions

As the swirling energy of the moment settles, you take a deep breath and look inward, gathering your thoughts. The compass glows softly in your hand, its light reflecting a desire for understanding. You realize this is your moment to seek clarity—to ask the questions that have been forming in your mind.

The voice of the ethereal being returns, calm and steady:

“Questions hold great power. To ask is to open a door. To listen is to walk through it. What is it you wish to know?”

The air around you feels charged, as though the very fabric of the universe awaits your inquiry. You gather your courage and begin to ask.

What will you ask?

What is my purpose on this journey? What truths are hidden within me? Which path will lead me to the light?
