Chapter 3: Follow the Path to the Void

The path before you stretches into darkness, the trees thinning until only shadows remain. The air grows cold and still, and the compass in your hand hums faintly, its light dim but unwavering. With each step, the world around you becomes quieter, emptier, until you feel as though you are walking into nothingness itself.

The void is not hostile, but vast—an infinite expanse of silence and potential. As you venture deeper, the voice of the unseen resonates softly in your mind:

“The void is where all things begin and end. To walk this path is to embrace both creation and dissolution. What will you find in the emptiness?”

Ahead, three faint glimmers appear, like stars in the infinite darkness. Each one pulls at your soul, offering a different truth to uncover.

Which glimmer will you follow?

The glimmer of creation: forge something new from nothing. The glimmer of knowledge: uncover hidden truths in the void. The glimmer of dissolution: release what no longer serves you.
