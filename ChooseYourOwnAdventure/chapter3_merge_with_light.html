Chapter 3: Merge with the Light

You step into the radiant light, its warmth enveloping you completely. The boundaries of your body dissolve as the light flows through you, merging with your essence. It feels infinite yet intimate, as though you have returned to a source you’ve always known.

Visions of the universe unfold before you—planets spinning, stars forming, and galaxies dancing in harmony. The light speaks without words, conveying truths that resonate within your core. A voice, soft yet powerful, whispers:

“To merge with the light is to embrace your true self. Here, there is no fear, no separation—only unity and understanding.”

The light pulses gently, offering you a choice on how to integrate this experience into your journey.

How will you proceed?

Expand the light within and carry it forward. Seek deeper cosmic truths within the light. Return to your journey, transformed by the light.
