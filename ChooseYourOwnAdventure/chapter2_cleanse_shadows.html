Chapter 2: Cleanse the Shadows

The shaman’s chanting deepens, resonating with the rhythm of your heartbeat. Sacred smoke swirls around you, weaving intricate patterns in the air. You feel the weight of the shadows within you—your fears, doubts, and past regrets—beginning to stir.

“The shadows do not leave willingly,” the shaman intones. “But they can be cleansed with intention and courage. Let the light within you rise and purify what has been hidden.”

A warmth begins to grow in your chest, spreading through your body. The shadows inside you writhe and resist, but as the light grows stronger, they begin to dissolve, releasing whispers of long-forgotten memories.

“Do not fight the shadows,” the shaman says. “Acknowledge them, honor them, and let them transform.”

As the cleansing continues, you must decide what to focus on:

Focus on releasing pain from your past. Clear the doubts clouding your present path. Embrace the purity of the light and its transformative power.
