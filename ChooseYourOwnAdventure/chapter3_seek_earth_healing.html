Chapter 3: Seek Earth Healing

The forest seems to breathe with life as you kneel and place your hands on the soil. The warmth of the Earth flows through you, and you feel its energy—a combination of resilience, pain, and renewal. The Earth is alive, its heartbeat strong, yet you sense its wounds as well.

Closing your eyes, you focus on the connection between yourself and the Earth. A soft voice, deep and ancient, rises from the ground:

“To heal the Earth is to heal yourself. Its cycles are your cycles. Where will you place your intention?”

The compass in your hand glows green, resonating with the vitality of the land. The energy of the Earth invites you to take action.

How will you engage with the Earth’s healing?

Channel your energy to help heal the Earth. Learn from the Earth’s natural cycles of renewal. Gather wisdom from the Earth to guide your path.
