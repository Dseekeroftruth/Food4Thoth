Chapter 2: Embrace the Light

You close your eyes, taking a deep breath, and focus on the warm glow within. The rhythm of the shaman’s drum fades into the background, replaced by a radiant pulse of light filling your mind and body.

Golden streams of energy flow around you, dissolving the shadows. You feel weightless, as if lifted by an unseen force. The light grows brighter, transforming into a shimmering sphere that encircles you in perfect serenity.

“The light within you is eternal,” a gentle voice says. “It connects you to all that is and all that will be. Embrace it, and you will find your path.”

The light reveals choices before you:

Merge fully with the light and transcend. Carry the light back to your physical self. Explore the origin and source of the light.
