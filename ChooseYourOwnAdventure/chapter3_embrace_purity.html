Chapter 3: Embrace Purity

In the silence of the forest, a radiant light envelops you. The compass in your hand grows warm, its glow soft yet profound. The world seems to hold its breath as you sense a call to let go—to release all fear, doubt, and resistance. You stand in stillness, allowing the light to flow through you.

Visions of clarity and simplicity begin to emerge. The noise of the journey fades, replaced by a deep understanding of what truly matters. A voice, gentle yet firm, speaks within:

“Purity is not perfection—it is truth. To embrace purity is to let go of all that is not you.”

The light brightens, offering you a choice: to move forward unburdened, to seek deeper understanding, or to share this clarity with others.

How will you embrace purity?

Move forward with clarity and purpose. Seek deeper truths about your nature. Share this newfound clarity with others.
