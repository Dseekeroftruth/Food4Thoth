You sit down with the compass, its alien glyphs glowing faintly. Taking a deep breath, you focus on the strange symbols, their shapes shifting as though alive. “These symbols are the language of the unseen,” whispers a voice within you. “They unlock the hidden pathways of existence. What do you seek to understand?”

What do you focus on?

Try to unlock the true power of the compass. Decipher the glyphs’ meaning and purpose. Let your instinct guide you without understanding.
