Chapter 3: Seek Life Purpose

You sit in stillness, the world around you fading into silence. The compass rests in your lap, glowing faintly, its needle pointing inward. Closing your eyes, you let your thoughts dissolve, allowing the whispers of the universe to surface.

Visions begin to form—flashes of moments from your past, choices you’ve made, and the threads that bind them together. You sense the vast web of existence, and within it, your unique thread. A voice, calm and resonant, speaks within you:

“The purpose you seek is not found—it is created. It lives in your actions, your choices, and the way you carry the light and shadow within.”

With this insight, the compass pulses, offering clarity. You feel a pull toward decisions that may define your purpose and shape your path forward.

How will you explore your purpose?

Reflect on past choices to understand your purpose. Define a future path aligned with your values. Embrace the present moment as your purpose.
