# 🌟 Sacred Geometry Food4Thoth Tarot

The Sacred Geometry Food4Thoth Tarot is an interactive digital tarot experience inspired by sacred geometry and deep esoteric wisdom. This project expands upon an original tarot interface to create a dynamic, visually immersive divination tool designed for self-reflection, spiritual growth, and intuitive guidance.

# 🃏 Overview

This interactive tarot experience allows users to engage with all 78 cards of the Tarot deck, categorized into:
1. Full Deck Mode – Explore all 78 cards.
2. Major Arcana Mode – Focus on the 22 Major Arcana for deep spiritual insights.
3. Suit-Specific Modes – Choose between Cups, Wands, Swords, and Pentacles, each featuring all Ace through King cards.

Each card displays:
- Card name and number
- Unique sacred geometry-inspired imagery
- A fluid, interactive card flip animation

---

# 🎨 Features

## 🔮 Interactive Tarot Readings
- Click on cards to reveal their Name.
- Immersive animations create a fluid, organic experience.
- Dynamic scrolling & perspective shifts enhance the aesthetic.

## 🌙 Dark Mode Support
- Toggle between light and dark themes for comfort and accessibility.
- Smooth transitions ensure an engaging user experience.

## 🎵 Ambient Audio Integration
- Activate “Sonar Vibes” for a meditative soundscape.
- Looping BlipBlox-inspired ambient tones enhance focus and atmosphere.

## 🔗 Modular Navigation
- Expandable menus for Tarot, Community Projects, Games, and Sound Visualizers.
- Quick access to Food4Thoth’s Black Business Directory, Anarchy Archives, and more.

## 📜 Sacred Geometry Theme
- Each tarot card features intricate geometric designs.
- Symbolism aligns with traditional interpretations, enhanced with a futuristic aesthetic.

## 📱 Responsive & Mobile-Friendly
- Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile users.
- Smooth scrolling and responsive touch interactions.

---

# 🚀 How to Use

1. **Choose a Tarot Mode**   
      • Full Deck – View all 78 cards.   
	    •	Major Arcana – Dive into the 22 Major  Arcana.    
    	•	Suit-Specific – Focus on Cups, Wands, Swords, or  Pentacles.

2. **Flip a Card**    
	    •	Click or tap on a card to reveal its meaning.     
	    •	The flip animation brings an interactive element to the reading.     

3. **Toggle Dark Mode** (Optional)   
	•	Click the 🌙 Dark Mode button for a darker, high-contrast experience.

4. **Explore Additional Features**   
	•	Navigate to other Food4Thoth projects including games, sound visualizers, and esoteric tools.

# 🛠️ Technologies Used
- Frontend:    
  • HTML5 – Structured content.    
	•	CSS3 – Styling and dark mode.    
	•	JavaScript (ES6, Vue.js) – Dynamic tarot.    interactions.    
	•	Animations & Effects:    
	•	CSS transitions & perspective transformations for smooth card flips.    
	•	Parallax scrolling & kaleidoscope background effects.     
	•	Sound Integration:     
	•	JavaScript-based audio toggle for immersive meditation.     
	•	Looping BlipBlox ambient track.     
	•	Navigation & UI:    
	•	Expandable menu system for streamlined exploration.    
	•	Neumorphic UI design for a modern aesthetic.
