How It Works

From Question to Hexagram

A reading takes less than a minute. The depth of reflection it offers can last much longer.

1

Hold your question

Form a clear, sincere question in your mind. The I Ching responds best to genuine seeking — decisions, relationships, transitions, timing, or inner state.

2

Choose your method

Select Coin Toss, Manual, Fractal, or Photo, depending on the edition. Each channel gives the question a different creative texture.

3

Cast your six lines

The app builds your hexagram line by line — solid yang or broken yin — including changing lines that point toward transformation.

4

Read the oracle's response

Judgement, Image, changing-line meanings, and transformed hexagram details are presented clearly for interpretation and reflection.

5

Save, export, or reflect

Save the reading to your journal, export it as an image or PDF where available, or return to your history to notice patterns over time.
