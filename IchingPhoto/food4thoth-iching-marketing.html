Four Paths to Wisdom

Choose Your Oracle Method

Whether you trust spontaneity, deliberate intention, visual art, or personal memory — every path resolves to the same 64-hexagram wisdom tradition.

🔮

Coin Toss

A digital recreation of the classical three-coin method. Cast all six lines instantly or build them one at a time for a more deliberate pace. PRO adds 3D animated coins and authentic coin toss sound effects for a fully tactile experience.

✍️

Manual Input

Select each line by hand — solid, broken, or changing — for complete deliberate control. Choose solid (yang), broken (yin), old yang (changing yang), or old yin (changing yin). Suited to practitioners who bring their own interpretation to the hexagram's construction.

🌌

Fractal Oracle

Draw on the canvas and let generative fractal art translate your strokes into hexagram lines. PRO includes 14 modes across two collections — Classic (Flow Field, Spiral, Tree, Julia Set, Labyrinth, Mandelbrot, Barnsley Fern) and Vivid (Rainbow, Neon, Psychedelic, Plasma, Galaxy, Prism, Fire).

📷

Photo Oracle

Select any photo from your device. The app analyzes its color composition entirely on-device to generate a hexagram unique to that exact image — every photo tells a different story. PRO lets you export the reading alongside your source photo as a shareable image.

How It Works

From Question to Hexagram

A reading takes less than a minute. The depth of reflection it offers can last a lifetime.

1

Hold your question

Form a clear, sincere question in your mind. The I Ching responds best to genuine seeking — matters of relationship, decision, direction, or inner state.

2

Choose your method

Select Coin Toss, Manual, Fractal, or Photo. Each method channels the question differently — choose the one that feels most resonant.

3

Cast your six lines

The app builds your hexagram line by line — solid (yang) or broken (yin) — including any changing lines that point to a transformed second hexagram.

4

Read the oracle's response

The Judgement, Image, and applicable changing-line meanings are presented clearly. The transformed hexagram, if any, reveals where the situation is moving.

5

Save, export, or reflect

Save the reading to your personal journal, export it as a styled image to share, or simply sit with the wisdom. PRO tracks patterns across all your readings over time.
