How It Works

From Question to Hexagram

A reading takes less than a minute. The depth of reflection it offers can last a lifetime.

1

Hold your question

Form a clear, sincere question in your mind. The I Ching responds best to genuine seeking — matters of relationship, decision, direction, or inner state.

2

Choose your method

Select Coin Toss, Manual, Fractal, or Photo. Each method channels the question differently — choose the one that feels most resonant.

3

Cast your six lines

The app builds your hexagram line by line — solid (yang) or broken (yin) — including any changing lines that point to a transformed second hexagram.

4

Read the oracle's response

The Judgement, Image, and applicable changing-line meanings are presented clearly. The transformed hexagram, if any, reveals where the situation is moving.

5

Save, export, or reflect

Save the reading to your personal journal, export it as a styled image or PDF, or simply sit with the wisdom. PRO and Elite track patterns across all your readings over time.

Wilhelm / Baynes · 1950
The Classic

Richard Wilhelm's German translation, rendered into English by Cary F. Baynes with a foreword by C.G. Jung. The definitive Western I Ching for over 70 years.

"The Creative works sublime success, furthering through perseverance."
James Legge · 1899
The Scholar

The first complete scholarly English translation of the I Ching — precise, literal, and deeply rooted in Chinese classical tradition. Public domain.

"The first hexagram is all formed of undivided lines. Its attributes are strength and creativity."
Contemporary · New
The Modern Voice

A fresh, accessible rendering for the modern reader — clear language, contemporary insight, without losing the depth and nuance of the source.

"Heaven's force flows pure and strong. Act with integrity and everything aligns — lasting success comes through sustained right action."
