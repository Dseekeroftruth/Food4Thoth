The Neon Serpent Spiral
Privacy Policy
Effective date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: January 1, 2025
◈ The Short Version
The Neon Serpent Spiral does not collect any personal data. It does not track you, connect to the internet, or share anything with anyone. Your game progress lives only on your device. That is the complete summary. Everything below explains this in more detail for those who need it.
No Data Collected
We collect zero personal information. No name, email, device ID, or usage statistics.
No Internet Required
The app runs fully offline after download. No network connections are made during gameplay.
No Analytics
No third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or crash reporting tools are included in the app.
No Advertising
No ads. No ad networks. No advertising identifiers are accessed or used.
01
Who We Are
The Neon Serpent Spiral is an iOS game created by DeJahn of Food4Thoth. This privacy policy applies to the iOS application available on the Apple App Store (App ID: 6760896039) and the web version available at this website.
Contact: food4thoth@proton.me
02
Information We Collect
We collect no information.
The Neon Serpent Spiral does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal information or usage data of any kind. This includes but is not limited to:
· Your name, email address, or any contact information
· Your device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, or similar)
· Your location data
· Your IP address
· Your gameplay behavior, session length, or usage patterns
· Crash reports or diagnostic data
· Any information you type or interact with inside the app
03
Local Storage
The app saves your game progress (current zone, inventory, karma score, weirdness score, and settings preferences) locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.
Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
04
Audio & Microphone
All sound in The Neon Serpent Spiral is generated synthetically on your device using the device's audio output. No microphone access is requested or used. The app does not record, process, or transmit any audio input.
If the expo-audio module included in the app requests a microphone permission at the system level, this is a requirement of the module framework and does not reflect actual microphone usage — the app does not use the microphone under any circumstances.
05
Third-Party Services
The Neon Serpent Spiral does not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or data brokers.
The app is built using the Expo / React Native framework. Expo's standard runtime does not collect user data from apps that do not configure data collection services. No such services are configured in The Neon Serpent Spiral.
06
Children's Privacy
The Neon Serpent Spiral is rated 4+ on the Apple App Store and is appropriate for all ages. Because the app collects no data from any users, it also collects no data from children. We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international children's privacy laws.
07
Apple App Store
Downloading the app through the Apple App Store is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and terms of service. Apple may collect certain data related to your App Store account and download activity according to their policies, which are separate from and independent of this policy.
Apple's Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
08
Changes to This Policy
If this privacy policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Because we collect no data, any changes are unlikely to affect your privacy in a meaningful way — but we will be transparent about any changes regardless.
09
Your Rights
Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. Your game data exists solely on your device and is entirely within your control.
If you have any privacy-related questions or concerns, you are welcome to contact us at the address below and we will respond promptly.
