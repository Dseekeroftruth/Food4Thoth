# Neon Nudge: The Iridescent Afterparty

A self-contained PG-13 cyberpunk rainbow point-and-click adventure inspired by cheeky old-school adventure games, with flirty banter, double entendres, rooms, NPCs, dialogue choices, inventory, puzzles, generated synth audio, unlockable areas, and a final afterparty ending.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Works on desktop and mobile/iPhone touch screens.

- Tap glowing objects to inspect or collect items.
- Talk to NPCs for branching dialogue and puzzle hints.
- Collect inventory items and use them to earn Consent Crystals.
- Unlock rooms with required items.
- Collect seven crystals to enter the Iridescent Afterparty.
- Tap Sound to start browser-generated music.
- Save/Load uses localStorage.

## Content tone

This is suggestive but not explicit: PG-13 innuendo, cheeky double entendres, flirtation, surreal fairy/cyberpunk comedy, and consent-positive puzzle design.
