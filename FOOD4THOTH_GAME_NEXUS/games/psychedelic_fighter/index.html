RULES OF THE TRIP

Best of 3 rounds. 60 seconds each. KO or most-health-at-time wins.

Specials cost meter, which fills as you take and deal damage.

In Tournament, win 10 fights to face The All-Seeing Eye. Defeat the boss to unlock them.
