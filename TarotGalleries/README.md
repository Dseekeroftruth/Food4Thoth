# FOOD4THOTH Tarot Galleries

## 📜 Project Overview

**FOOD4THOTH Tarot Galleries** is a dynamic and immersive digital platform that showcases 312 original tarot cards across four unique decks. It serves as a portal for esoteric exploration, sacred geometry, cyberpunk aesthetics, and interactive divination tools. The project is a fusion of mysticism, technology, and creative storytelling.

---

# ✨ Key Features:
- **Interactive Tarot Galleries** – Explore Cyberpunk Dark, Cyberpunk Melo, Sacred Geometry, and OG Traditional tarot decks.
- **Neumorphic UI Navigation** – Provides an intuitive and futuristic interface for seamless browsing.
- **Live Previews & Interactive Tarot Apps** – Experience tarot readings and dynamic visualizations.
- **Multimedia & Sound Visualizers** – Sound-activated visualizers enhance the mystical experience.
- **Community & Creative Integration** – Links to Black Business Directories, Community Gardens, Esoteric Tools, and Anarchy & Hashisheen Reviews.

---

# 🖼️ Gallery Sections

# 🔮 FOOD4THOTH Tarot Card Gallery
- Explore 312 original tarot cards across four visionary decks:

1. **Cyberpunk Dark** – AI surveillance, cybernetic oppression, and rebellion.
2. **Cyberpunk Melo** – Surreal cyber-emotions and digital intimacy.
3. **Sacred Geometry** – Divine patterns and universal symbols.
4. **OG Traditional** – A modern take on classic tarot archetypes.

---

# ✨ Majix Galleries
- An interactive digital tarot experience inspired by sacred geometry, esoteric wisdom, and intuitive divination.
- Features immersive animations, sacred geometry visuals, and tarot-based soundscapes.

---

# 🌀 Sacred Geometry Tarot Flip-Style Gallery
- Browse 78 tarot cards in an animated flip-style format.
- Quick reference for Upright & Reversed Meanings, Astrological Associations, and Elemental Influences.

---

# 🔥 FOOD4THOTH 18+ Tarot Gallery
- **Crystal Queens Tarot** – A sensual, divine feminine deck infused with mysticism and erotic power.
- **Cyberpunk-X Tarot** – Digital intimacy, transhumanist rebellion, and cybernetic transformation.

# 🛠️ Technologies Used
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript – Core web technologies for interactivity and animations.
- Neumorphic Design – A futuristic soft UI aesthetic.
- Google Fonts (Uncial Antiqua) – Adds a mystical feel.
- Smooth Scrolling & Navigation Scripts – Enhances user experience.
- Embedded Sound & Visual Elements – Dynamic tarot experiences.
