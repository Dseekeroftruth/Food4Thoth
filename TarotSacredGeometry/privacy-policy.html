Who We Are

FOOD4THOTH Sacred Geometry Tarot is a web-based tarot, sacred geometry, sound, breathwork, meditation, journaling, and symbolic ritual experience published as part of FOOD4THOTH.com.

FOOD4THOTH is an independent creative project connected with Artabillies, building experimental webpages, sacred arts tools, music portals, educational resources, games, and community-centered digital experiences.

For privacy questions, support questions, or website feedback, contact: food4thoth@proton.me.

03

Information We Do Not Ask You to Provide

You can use the Sacred Geometry Tarot web oracle without creating an account, signing in, or submitting personal information directly to us.

We do not ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your email address to use the oracle
  • Your mailing address
  • Your phone number
  • Your precise location
  • Your payment information to use the free web oracle
  • Your contacts
  • Your microphone recordings
  • Your camera access for normal website use
The Sacred Geometry Tarot web oracle is intended to be usable as a free browser-based experience without checkout, login, or account creation.

Like most websites, FOOD4THOTH.com may be served through web hosting, domain, browser, network, or security infrastructure that can automatically process limited technical information when you visit a page.

This technical information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring page
  • Pages requested
  • Date and time of access
  • Basic error or security logs

This type of information is generally used for website delivery, troubleshooting, security, performance, and abuse prevention. FOOD4THOTH does not use this information to personally profile visitors or sell visitor data.

05

Local Browser Storage

Some features of the Sacred Geometry Tarot web oracle may save information locally in your browser using technologies such as localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or similar browser-based storage.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Saved tarot readings
  • Daily draw history
  • Favorite cards
  • Journal-style reflections or notes
  • Breathwork or meditation preferences
  • Sound on/off settings
  • Interface preferences
  • Saved spread choices or ritual practice details

This information is intended to stay in your own browser on your own device. FOOD4THOTH does not require this information to be uploaded to an account or sent to a FOOD4THOTH user database.

You can usually delete this locally stored information by clearing your browser data for FOOD4THOTH.com or using any reset/clear options provided inside the webpage.

Cookies, Fonts, and Embedded Resources

The Sacred Geometry Tarot pages may use standard web technologies to display the website correctly. This may include stylesheets, images, icons, scripts, fonts, or embedded resources.

Browser settings, privacy extensions, or content blockers may affect how fonts, media, sounds, or saved local features work.

10

Data Retention and Deletion

Information saved locally by the tarot webpage remains in your browser until you clear it, overwrite it, reset the webpage storage, or remove the relevant browser data.

You may be able to delete local data by:

  • Using clear/reset options inside the web oracle, if available
  • Clearing browser storage for FOOD4THOTH.com
  • Clearing cookies and site data in your browser settings
  • Using private browsing mode to avoid long-term local storage

FOOD4THOTH cannot delete local browser data from your device on your behalf because that information is controlled by your own browser and device.
