Who We Are

FOOD4THOTH Sacred Tarot ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is an application for iOS and Android developed and published by FOOD4THOTH, operated by the individual developer at dseekeroftruth@gmail.com.

The App is a divination and personal reflection tool featuring 78 sacred geometry tarot cards, daily draws, spread readings, and a personal journal. It is available as a $9.99 one-time purchase on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how any on-device data is used, and your rights in relation to your information. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be completely clear: FOOD4THOTH Sacred Tarot does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information from or about you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, Android Advertising ID, or similar)
  • Usage data, analytics, or behavioral tracking
  • Crash reports sent to any external service
  • Purchase or payment information beyond standard store processing handled by Apple or Google
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Any content you write in the Sacred Journal feature
The App contains no analytics SDK, no advertising network, and no third-party data collection library of any kind.

03

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party. The data stored locally includes:

  • Journal notes: Any personal reflections you write on individual cards
  • Favorites: The list of cards you have bookmarked
  • Daily draw history: Which card was drawn on the current day, and whether it was reversed
  • Onboarding status: Whether you have completed the first-launch introduction (a simple true/false value)

This data is stored using local device storage APIs and is subject to your device's standard security protections, including encryption and app sandboxing where supported by your platform. If you delete the App, all locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

Children's Privacy

FOOD4THOTH Sacred Tarot is designed to be suitable for general audiences. Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, there are no special provisions required for users under the age of 13 (COPPA) or under the age of 16 (GDPR).

Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data to our own servers, there is no personal information stored by us on a remote user database.

Data stored locally on your device (journal notes, favorites, and daily draw history) benefits from your device's built-in security features, which may include:

Your Rights

Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on our end. All data associated with your use of the App exists solely on your device and is entirely under your control.

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Right to erasure: Delete the App from your device. All locally stored data will be permanently removed.
  • Right to access: All data the App stores is directly accessible within the App itself (your journal notes, favorites, and daily draw).
  • Right to portability: Your journal notes are text you have written and remain entirely yours.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA respectively. Because we collect no personal data, these rights are inherently fulfilled — there is no data to access, export, or delete on our servers.
