Who We Are

FOOD4THOTH Sacred Tarot ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is an application developed and published by FOOD4THOTH.

The App is a divination and personal reflection tool featuring 78 sacred geometry tarot cards, daily draws, spread readings, sacred geometry exploration, sound healing, breathwork, and personal journaling. It is available as a $9.99 one-time purchase on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. Android is planned for a future release and is not yet available.

For privacy questions or concerns, contact us at food4thothdotcom@gmail.com.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be completely clear: FOOD4THOTH Sacred Tarot does not collect, transmit, store, or process personal information about you on our servers.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information through the app itself
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device identifiers such as IDFA, IDFV, or Android Advertising ID
  • Usage data, analytics, or behavioral tracking
  • Crash reports sent to any third-party crash service
  • Purchase or payment details beyond platform-level store processing handled by Apple or Google
  • Biometric data
  • Your contacts, microphone data, or personal files
  • The contents of your journal entries, saved spreads, ritual notes, favorites, or symbol work
The App includes no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party data collection library of any kind.

03

Device Permissions

The App may request certain optional system permissions depending on which features you choose to use. These permissions are used only for their stated in-app purpose, and resulting data is not transmitted to us.

Permission Why it’s needed When requested
Camera Optional camera overlay for symbol creation or visual reference features, if enabled in the app. Only when you choose to use a camera-based feature. Never required for basic app use.
Photo Library (Write) Saving exported symbol art, oracle card images, or similar user-created visuals to your device. Only when you tap an export or save-to-device action.
Audio (Playback) Binaural tones, meditation sounds, pathworking audio, or breathwork sound playback while using the app. Used only when sound features are enabled by you. No recording occurs.

No microphone audio is recorded or transmitted. The app’s sound features generate or play sound locally on your device only.

04

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores a limited amount of data locally on your device only using standard device storage. This information is not sent to us and is not shared with third parties.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored data may include:

  • Journal notes: Personal reflections attached to cards or rituals
  • Favorites: Cards or content you bookmark
  • Daily draw history: The card drawn that day, including reversed state where applicable
  • Reading history: Spreads used, timestamps, or previously drawn cards
  • Practice history: Geometry views, pathworking progress, breathwork usage, or ritual counts
  • Custom spreads: Saved layouts or personal spread configurations
  • Personal symbols: Drawings, names, or meanings you create in symbol features
  • App preferences: Settings such as sound on/off, onboarding state, or interface preferences

This data is stored using your device’s normal application storage environment and remains under your control. If you delete the App, locally stored data is deleted from your device unless it is preserved through a platform backup feature you have personally enabled.

Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on remote servers, there is no user database maintained by us containing your private in-app content.

Data stored locally on your device benefits from your platform’s built-in protections, which may include:

  • App sandboxing
  • Device passcode and operating-system security controls
  • Device-level encryption where supported by your platform
  • Optional Apple or Google cloud backup systems that you personally enable

If you use device backup features such as iCloud Backup or future Android backup services, locally stored app data may be included in your personal backup account, subject to the privacy and security practices of that platform.

09

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all app data is stored locally on your device, you retain direct control over it.

  • Delete specific records: Some items may be removable directly within the app interface.
  • Delete all app data: Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data from your device, subject to any backup systems you have enabled.
  • Backups: If app data has been backed up through your own cloud settings, managing those backups is controlled through your Apple or Google account settings.

We cannot delete data on your behalf because we do not possess it.
