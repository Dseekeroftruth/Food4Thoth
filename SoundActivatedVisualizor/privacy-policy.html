Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 — Sonic Visualizer by FOOD4THOTH

Microphone Access

Sonic Visualizer uses your device's microphone solely to create real-time visual animations that react to the sounds and music around you.

We do not:

  • Record any audio
  • Store any audio data
  • Transmit any audio data to any server
  • Share any audio data with any third party

Microphone input is processed entirely on your device in real time and is discarded immediately after each visual frame is rendered.

Data Collection

Sonic Visualizer collects no personal data of any kind. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, and no tracking.

Third-Party Services

Sonic Visualizer does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Sonic Visualizer does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Where to Get Sonic Visualizer

This same privacy commitment applies everywhere Sonic Visualizer runs — on iOS and in the browser:

  • iOS app on the App Store
  • Web app (newest) — runs in your browser
  • Classic web app
  • Sonic Visualizer home

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact: food4thoth@proton.me

Changes

Any future changes to this policy will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
