Ground Cherry – FOOD4THOTH Music Showcase

Overview

Ground Cherry is an electrifying garage-psych trio delivering hypnotic sonic journeys that blend surf rock, stoner grooves, post-punk, and modal riffs. This digital showcase includes live performances, visual galleries, and video loops wrapped in a glitchy, immersive UI inspired by cyberpunk and underground aesthetics.

This landing page was designed as part of the FOOD4THOTH music and visual experiment ecosystem, merging performance, storytelling, and digital art.

⸻

🎸 Ground Cherry Sound

Described As:
	•	Garage Psych × Stoner Rock × Post-Punk
	•	Influences: Thee Oh Sees, Khruangbin, early Sonic Youth
	•	Features:
	•	Guitar: Jangly, overdriven, heavily textured.
	•	Bass: Melodic, syncopated, front-and-center.
	•	Drums: Motorik pulse, jazz-kraut groove hybrids.
	•	A fusion of minimalism and movement — stripped-down yet sonically full.

⸻

🎥 Features

Glitch Art + Parallax Gallery
	•	Full-width glitch-text title using layered CSS animation.
	•	Parallax visual backgrounds and neon glitch overlays.
	•	Scroll-triggered transitions to music, video, and info zones.

Visual Slideshow
	•	Full-image glitch photo gallery
	•	Transitions, hover effects, and manual navigation

Embedded Video Showcases
	•	Blue Bell House Experiments
	•	Ground Cherry First – Third Shows
	•	Full YouTube Playlist Integration

Extended Scene Support
	•	Linked entries for Peruvian Melt, Abyss of Bliss, INPROGRESSION, and DeJahn’s solo work.

⸻

🎧 How to Use

1. View & Scroll
	•	Scroll down to view glitch galleries and read Ground Cherry’s full philosophy.

2. Watch Embedded Videos
	•	Facebook and YouTube embeds load in iframe sections. Click play to stream.

3. Explore Related Bands
	•	Links to related Food4Thoth projects and artists appear near the embedded sections.

4. Navigate the Portal
	•	Use the toggleable sticky menu to jump between music apps, games, tarot tools, and more.

⸻

💻 Technologies Used
	•	HTML5 / CSS3 – Parallax visuals, animated glitch text
	•	JavaScript – Gallery slide transitions, dynamic menu toggling
	•	Facebook & YouTube Embeds – Direct stream of videos
	•	Custom Font & Effects – Syne Mono + color-shifting backgrounds
