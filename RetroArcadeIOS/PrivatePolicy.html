You may choose to provide information when you:

This may include your email address and the contents of your message.

B. Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, certain information may be processed automatically to keep the Service running and secure:

We use this information for reliability, debugging, performance, and security.

Retro Arcade may store game settings, scores, or preferences locally using browser storage (like localStorage) so your experience works smoothly. This data stays on your device unless you clear your browser/app storage.

5) Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality (like keeping settings). Some third-party services (if enabled) may also use cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retro Arcade is not directed to children under 13 (or the age defined by your local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.
