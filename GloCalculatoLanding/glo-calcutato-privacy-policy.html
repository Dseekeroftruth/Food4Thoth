1 Who We Are

Glo-CALCULATO is developed and published by DeJahn Bell / FOOD4THOTH.

Website: food4thoth.com
App Name: Glo-CALCULATO
Platform: iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store)

2 Information We Collect

Glo-CALCULATO is a calculator. The core app does not collect any personal information from you. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photos.
  • We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
The only data processing that occurs is through optional in-app purchases, handled entirely by RevenueCat and the platform store (Apple / Google). See Section 4.

3 How We Use Information

Since we collect no personal data directly, there is nothing for us to use, share, or sell. Your calculations remain entirely on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

If you choose to make a donation through the app, the transaction is processed securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and RevenueCat. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made — not your payment details.

4 In-App Purchases & RevenueCat

Glo-CALCULATO offers optional one-time donation tiers through in-app purchases. These purchases are powered by RevenueCat, a third-party purchase management platform.

When you make a purchase, RevenueCat may process:

  • A randomized anonymous user identifier (generated automatically)
  • Purchase receipt data provided by Apple or Google
  • Device type and operating system version (for technical purposes only)
Important: RevenueCat does not receive your real name, billing address, or payment card details. That information is handled exclusively by Apple or Google as part of their store purchase flow.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at: revenuecat.com/privacy

All payment transactions are subject to:

  • Apple App Store Terms & Privacy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Play Terms & Privacy: policies.google.com/privacy

5 Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may be used by the app. Each has its own privacy policy independent of ours:

  • RevenueCat — In-app purchase management (privacy policy)
  • Apple App Store — iOS distribution and payments (privacy policy)
  • Google Play Store — Android distribution and payments (privacy policy)
  • Expo / React Native — App framework (privacy policy)

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their policies directly.

6 Data Storage & Security

Glo-CALCULATO does not operate its own servers that store personal data. Your calculator inputs and results exist only in your device's memory while the app is running and are discarded when the app is closed.

Purchase status (whether you are a VIP Donor) is verified locally using a token provided by RevenueCat and stored securely on your device. This token does not contain personally identifiable information.

We take reasonable precautions to ensure the security of any data we do handle, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7 Children's Privacy

Glo-CALCULATO does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app does not require any account creation or personal information to use.

In-app purchases require a payment method, which is subject to age verification by Apple or Google as part of their store policies. We do not have control over or access to that process.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information.

8 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding any personal data that relates to you. Because Glo-CALCULATO collects no personal data directly, most of these rights apply to data held by third parties (RevenueCat, Apple, Google).

Rights you may have include:

  • Right to access — request a copy of data held about you
  • Right to deletion — request that data about you be deleted
  • Right to correction — request that inaccurate data be corrected
  • Right to opt out — opt out of certain data processing activities

To exercise rights related to RevenueCat data, contact: revenuecat.com/privacy.
To exercise rights related to Apple, visit: privacy.apple.com.
To exercise rights related to Google, visit: myaccount.google.com.

For any questions directed to us personally, use the contact information in Section 10.

9 Changes to This Policy

Continued use of Glo-CALCULATO after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
