Why Glo-CALCULATO

Not just a calculator.
An experience.

Born from the creative universe of FOOD4THOTH, Glo-CALCULATO turns every calculation into a colorful, living moment. Rotating rainbow gradients wrap every button and the display — because math should feel magical.

🌈
Rotating Rainbow Borders
Every button and the calculator body are wrapped in a continuously rotating full-spectrum rainbow gradient. No two moments look alike.
😊
Smiley Face Friend
A hand-drawn smiley face greets you on launch. Press any key and it gently fades away. Tap AC and it comes back — happy to see you again.
🌀
Swirly Cosmic Background
A psychedelic, multi-layered swirling background fills your screen — always flowing, always alive, day or night.
📐
Full Scientific Math
Sin, cos, tan, log, ln, π, e, square roots, powers, parentheses — everything you need for homework, engineering, or the cosmos.
💚
Neon Green Results
Your answers glow in vivid neon green on a dark display — crisp, high-contrast, and satisfying every time you hit =.
📳
Haptic Feedback
Every button press delivers a precise haptic tap. The equals button gives a stronger pulse — that satisfying finish you can feel.
📋
Calculation History
Every result you calculate is saved in a scrollable history panel. Tap any past result to instantly load it back into the display.
🔄
DEG / RAD Toggle
Switch between Degrees and Radians with one tap. Trig functions automatically adjust — sin(90°) always gives 1 in DEG mode.
⎘
One-Tap Copy
Tap the copy icon on the display to copy any expression or result straight to your clipboard — ready to paste anywhere.
