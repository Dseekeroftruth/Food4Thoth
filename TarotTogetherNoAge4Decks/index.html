SACRED GEOMETRY

This Sacred Geometry Tarot Deck is a profound reimagining of the traditional Tarot, where each card is an abstract representation of cosmic principles through geometric forms. Rather than depicting literal figures or narrative scenes, the imagery focuses purely on Sacred Geometry, allowing the symbolism to emerge through patterns, structure, color, and composition.

Key Features of the Deck

  • Pure Geometry: No traditional human figures or objects—only geometric forms that convey archetypal forces.
  • Color Symbolism: Each card’s palette reinforces its meaning, with gradients and contrasts emphasizing energy shifts.
  • Symbolic Precision: Each shape is meticulously chosen to reflect its corresponding card’s deeper meaning.
  • Abstract, Meditative Aesthetic: Designed to engage the subconscious mind through sacred forms and archetypal resonance.

This Sacred Geometry Tarot Deck is an abstract, intuitive, and deeply symbolic system that transcends conventional imagery. By using the fundamental shapes of the universe, this deck taps into the language of creation itself. Whether used for meditation, divination, or philosophical reflection, each card invites the reader to explore the sacred mathematics that shape our existence.

CYBER-MELO

Cyberpunk Tarot Deck: MELO

Theme: A Fusion of Hope and Rebellion in a Neon-Lit Future

The MELO Cyberpunk Tarot Deck is a vibrant and evocative interpretation of traditional tarot, infused with the themes of technological enlightenment, digital spirituality, and rebellion against oppressive systems. Unlike a purely dystopian vision, this deck captures the balance between struggle and hope, showcasing a world where humanity fights to reclaim autonomy through technology, connection, and self-awareness.

Aesthetic and Design

  • Color Palette: Bright neon blues, purples, and pinks dominate the deck, representing digital transcendence and the energetic pulse of a city that never sleeps.
  • Art Style: A blend of anime-inspired cyberpunk with sleek digital aesthetics, mixing soft, painterly techniques with sharp, glowing holographic lines.
  • Symbols & Motifs: Neon circuitry, biotech integration, radiant orbs of energy, spiritual tattoos that function as digital enhancements, and augmented reality glyphs floating around the characters.

Themes & Interpretations

  • Technology as a Tool for Freedom: The deck explores how hacking, artificial intelligence, and cybernetics can become means of spiritual awakening rather than just tools of oppression.
  • Digital Spirituality & Hope: Characters are synth-mystics, digital shamans, and cyber-oracles, using tech-based divination tools to uncover cosmic truths.
  • Resistance and Rebellion: The deck centers on underground communities of dreamers, artists, and rebels who use tech to overthrow corporate rule while preserving humanity’s soul.

Overall Mood & Purpose

The MELO Cyberpunk Tarot is a deck for those who seek hope in the darkness—for dreamers, rebels, and digital mystics. It speaks of spiritual resistance, community, and the power of technology to enlighten rather than enslave.

CYBER-DARK

Cyberpunk Tarot Deck: DARK

Theme: A Dystopian Future of Control and Surveillance

The DARK Cyberpunk Tarot Deck is a stark, haunting vision of a world where corporations, AI, and cybernetic enhancements have erased the last traces of human autonomy. This deck delves into surveillance capitalism, body modification gone too far, and the dangers of a fully digitized society.

Aesthetic and Design

  • Color Palette: Cold blacks, metallic silvers, deep reds, and blood-soaked neon hues dominate the deck.
  • Art Style: A mix of hyper-detailed dark cyberpunk with distorted glitch effects and unsettling biomechanical designs.
  • Symbols & Motifs: Hollow-eyed cyber-slaves, emotionless androids, corporate branding, barcodes on skin, bio-mechanical decay, and flickering glitching screens.

Themes & Interpretations

  • Technology as a Tool of Oppression: The deck shows how AI surveillance, corporate rule, and cybernetic dependency have destroyed individuality.
  • Loss of Identity & Humanity: Many figures are partially or fully digitized, their faces blank, their emotions stolen by software.
  • Corporate Corruption & Control: The deck focuses on a world where rebellion is crushed, and freedom is a fleeting illusion.

Overall Mood & Purpose

The DARK Cyberpunk Tarot is a deck for those who confront the shadows and are unafraid of the truth. It is a warning, a mirror to a potential future, and a tool for those who navigate the darkest aspects of reality.

It is for those who seek meaning in oppression, who resist, and who question the fate of humanity in an era of unchecked technology.

OG TRADITIONAL

Theme: A Timeless Journey with Sacred Geometry & Food4Thoth Flair

The OG Traditional Tarot Deck honors the classic Rider–Waite symbolism while reinterpreting each card through the lens of sacred geometry. Rich in tradition yet unmistakably modern, this deck weaves Food4Thoth’s creative energy into each image, creating a bridge between ancient archetypes and contemporary visual poetry.

Aesthetic and Design

  • Color Palette: Warm earth tones and deep jewel hues accented with flashes of sacred gold and vibrant geometric patterns.
  • Art Style: Rooted in the classic Rider–Waite style, the imagery is overlaid with precise, modern sacred geometry elements and Food4Thoth’s signature creative spark.
  • Symbols & Motifs: Traditional tarot symbols reimagined with intricate geometric patterns, cosmic grids, and subtle digital flourishes that evoke both mysticism and modernity.

Themes & Interpretations

  • Heritage Meets Innovation: Embracing the timeless archetypes of Rider–Waite while introducing new layers of symbolic geometry.
  • Divine Order and Transformation: Each card reflects the universal order through geometric precision, inviting the reader to explore both inner wisdom and external transformation.
  • Mysticism Reimagined: A balanced dialogue between ancient divination and contemporary artistic expression, crafted for the modern seeker.

Overall Mood & Purpose

The OG Traditional Tarot is designed for those who honor the past while looking forward to new insights. It serves as a guide, a meditative tool, and a work of art—inviting users to embark on a soulful journey that fuses time‑honored symbolism with a fresh, geometrically inspired vision.

Whether used for divination, meditation, or creative exploration, this deck offers a unique, multidimensional perspective on the journey of life.
