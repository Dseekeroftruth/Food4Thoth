# Empire’s Mirror: From Gaza to Georgia

*The Transnational Through Line of State Violence, Policing, and Social Warfare from the IDF to ICE*

---

## 📜 Overview

This investigative work examines the **shared tactics, technologies, and ideologies** binding together the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF), U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), and the increasingly militarized police infrastructure across American cities.  
Focusing on **2023–2025**, it uncovers how *occupation strategies abroad* are adapted for domestic repression, and how *domestic policing innovations* are exported internationally—creating a **feedback loop of state violence**.

The project links:
- **The Gaza Genocide** and siege warfare under the Netanyahu government.
- **ICE’s detention abuses** and “Alligator Alcatraz.”
- **Cop City** and paramilitary policing tactics.
- **Austerity economics** as a form of social control.
- The **corporate and military contractors** profiting from this security pipeline.
