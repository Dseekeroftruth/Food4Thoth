PRAYER FOR LOVE


We’re going to share a beautiful dream together!

It’s a warm sunny day. You hear birds, the gentle wind, and a little river. When you walk to the water’s edge, there sits an old man in meditation, radiant with colorful light.

"I asked my teacher the same question years ago…”

You ask him how he can shine with such a beautiful glow. He smiles kindly and begins to tell his story. Long ago, his teacher taught him how to share love by placing a special flame in his heart.

“The flame grew into a big, big fire…”

The flame the teacher placed in his heart began to grow. It became a fire that purified everything it touched. Soon, every cell in his body felt love. His mind and emotions were full of joy. He loved himself completely!

“I put a piece of love in all the plants and animals”

His heart yearned to share love. He placed a piece of his love into the plants and flowers, and they loved him back. His love touched animals, too, they gave it in return, they became one. His love grew and soon he felt united with all living things.

“I put my love in all the trees”

He gave love to all the trees, they gave it in return and they became one. His love grew.

“I put my love in water”

He gave love to rivers, rain, and snow, they gave it in return, they became one. His love grew.

“I put my love in air”

He sent love into the wind and felt communion with all of nature, it all gave it in return, they became one. His love grew.

“I put my love in earth”

He sent love into the earth and all the rocks, crystals, and minerals; it all gave it in return, they became one. His love grew.

“I put my love in sky”

Then he lifted his eyes to the sky, sharing love with the sun, moon, and stars. Which they all gave in return, they became one. His love grew and grew.

“I am a part of everything because I love.”

Finally, he shared love with every human he met, seeing himself in their eyes. He realized we are all part of one humanity.

“Now this flame is yours, too!”

The old man opens his chest, takes out his glowing heart-flame, and places it in your heart. You feel its warmth and light growing inside you, connecting you to the wind, water, stars—everything!

“Thank you, Creator, for this gift of life…”

You pray with gratitude for life, body, mind, and the boundless spirit of love inside you. You ask for help in keeping that love and peace in your heart always, so you may live in love for the rest of your life. Amen.
