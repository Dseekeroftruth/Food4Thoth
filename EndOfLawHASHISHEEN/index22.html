Please Enjoy the Review and Reading of this Track from Bill Laswell's Album Hashisheen

Combined and Complete Analysis of Track 22: “Last Reading”

Track Details:
• Title: Last Reading
• Duration: 0:37
• Read By: Sussan Deyhim

Full Lyrics:
• "Take one step outside yourself. The whole path lasts no longer than one step."

Overview:
• “Last Reading” concludes “Hashisheen: The End of Law”.
• The track is brief but profound, serving as both a reflection on the journey the listener has undertaken and an invitation for introspection.
• Delivered by the ethereal voice of Sussan Deyhim, it ties together the album’s overarching themes while offering a sense of closure.
• This track encapsulates the album’s philosophy in a minimalist and meditative format, emphasizing the simplicity and immediacy of enlightenment.
• The words and music, though sparse, carry immense weight, leaving a lasting impression on the listener.

Full Track Analysis:

Themes and Interpretations
• Closure and Reflection:
• The track synthesizes the album’s themes, creating a meditative space for the listener to process the journey’s insights.
• Mirrors the cyclical nature of the album, drawing parallels to the opening track, "First Reading."
• Suggests that the spiritual quest and personal growth are universal and ongoing processes.
• Reinforces the idea that endings are merely new beginnings.

• Mysticism and Spirituality:
• Encourages stepping beyond self-imposed limitations and embracing a broader understanding of existence.
• Aligns with mystical traditions emphasizing the journey inward as the ultimate path to enlightenment.
• Invites the listener to move beyond material and temporal attachments toward deeper truths.

• Impermanence and Continuity:
• Reflects on the transient nature of human experiences, while hinting at eternal truths.
• Suggests that growth and transformation are not bound by time.
• Acknowledges the lasting influence of ideas, teachings, and shared experiences, transcending physical limitations.

Lyrics Breakdown
• "Take one step outside yourself"
• Suggests shedding the ego, which limits self-awareness and perpetuates attachment to identity, fears, and societal constructs.
• Encourages a move toward detachment, freeing the individual to see themselves and the world from a more expansive perspective.
• Calls for a conscious decision to transcend the habitual self and its conditioned patterns.
• Reflects the beginning of self-realization, marking a departure from ignorance to understanding.

• "The whole path lasts no longer than one step"
• Reduces the spiritual journey to a single moment of choice or realization, demystifying the process.
• Emphasizes immediacy, suggesting that awakening occurs in the present and requires no prolonged journey.
• Suggests that all growth, insight, and transformation are encapsulated in the here and now.
• Highlights the non-linear nature of spiritual growth, where the first step contains the entire journey.
• Reflects the cyclical themes present throughout the album.

Sussan Deyhim’s Performance
• Deyhim’s voice is serene, profound, and evocative, perfectly suited to conveying the track’s meditative quality.
• The combination of her emotive inflections and measured cadence draws listeners into a contemplative state, leaving space for personal reflection.

Musical Composition
• The track is sparse, allowing the spoken word to take precedence and creating an intimate atmosphere.
• The music complements Deyhim’s voice, enhancing the lyrical weight without overshadowing it.
• Subtle shifts in tone and texture mirror the reflective themes.

Philosophical and Mystical Underpinnings
• Reflects the Sufi emphasis on the immediacy of divine union and the cyclical journey of self-discovery.
• Resonates with the philosophy that the seeker and the sought are one, and enlightenment is realizing this unity.
• Echoes Zen teachings on the simplicity of enlightenment, often described as "one step" or "one moment."

Emotional and Psychological Impact
• The track offers a calm and reflective end to the album, balancing the intensity of earlier pieces.
• By reducing the path to a single step, the track empowers the listener to embrace personal transformation.

Connection to the Album’s Themes
• The lyric mirrors the album’s exploration of beginnings and endings as interconnected points in a larger cycle.
• Encourages the listener to consider their own inner journey and the insights gained from the album.

Conclusion
• “Last Reading” encapsulates the essence of “Hashisheen: The End of Law.”
• Through the lyrics, "Take one step outside yourself. The whole path lasts no longer than one step," the track offers a profound message on the simplicity and immediacy of transformation.
• Sussan Deyhim’s serene delivery and the minimalist soundscape create an emotional and reflective experience, leaving a lasting impression on the listener.
• By uniting themes of mysticism, impermanence, and personal growth, “Last Reading” concludes the album with grace and depth.
• It invites the listener to carry its insights forward, encouraging a personal exploration of the journey toward enlightenment, which begins and ends in a single, transformative step.
