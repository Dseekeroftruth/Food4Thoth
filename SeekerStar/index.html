Band Members

DeJahn – “The Seeker”: Ethereal sounds, rhythms, spoken word, flute, hand drums, vocal FX, ambient textures, loops.

Laura Bella – “The Star”: Gorgeous melodic vocals, bass guitar, percussion, melodica, harmonic layering.



Musical Identity

Seeker and the Star is a Eugene-based two-piece crafting deeply intentional, ritualistic performances rooted in:


    Experimental Ambient Folk


    Soulful Downtempo


    Live Looping and Spoken Word


    Spiritual-Punk Expressionism

Seeker and the Star Is...


    Textural and immersive:

    Echoes of breath, wind, drum, and memory

    Melodic and raw:

    Laura’s vocals channel both serenity and urgency

    Rhythmic and evolving:

    Percussion drives movement, while synths and hand instruments ripple around it

    Sacred and honest:

    Every performance feels like a ceremony — vulnerable, real, and alive


Sonic Comparisons

In essence (not imitation), they evoke touches of Cocorosie, Portishead (Unplugged), Dead Can Dance, Massive Attack x Ritual Folk, and AURORA’s vocals + Ayahuasca rhythms.



Performance Description

You don’t watch Seeker and the Star — you enter it. A shared space where silence matters, where the drum is an ancestor, and where the voice becomes spell, memory, and movement.
