# 🐾 Trent of the Day: Son of Reznor

The **Trent of the Day** webpage is a heartfelt tribute to Trent, a beloved dog whose charm, spirit, and unique character left a lasting impression on everyone who met him. This interactive page celebrates Trent’s adventurous, love-filled life through engaging design, random quotes, and visual storytelling.

---

## 🌟 Overview

The **Trent of the Day** page offers:
- **Random Quotes and Images**: Each reload presents a unique quote and image of Trent, capturing his playful personality.
- **Interactive Features**: Smooth scrolling, toggleable views for galleries, and embedded Instagram posts.
- **Mobile-Responsive Design**: Ensures seamless functionality and aesthetics across devices.

This page is a tribute to the love, joy, and unforgettable moments Trent shared with his family and community.

---

## 🎨 Features

### **Interactive Elements**
- **Random Content**:
  - Displays a new image and quote on every reload for a fresh experience.
- **Scroll Buttons**:
  - Fixed buttons for quick navigation to the top or bottom of the page.
- **Toggleable Sections**:
  - Expand or collapse the photo gallery and social media links for a streamlined browsing experience.
	
---

### **Gallery**
- Showcases a wide array of Trent’s photos, highlighting his adventures and heartwarming moments.
- **Responsive Image Grid**: Optimized for various screen sizes.

---

### **Social Media Integration**
- **Instagram Embeds**:
  - Highlights Trent’s life, style, and adventures through a curated selection of posts.
  - Embedded links allow visitors to explore Trent’s story further.
	
---

### **Accessibility**
- **Keyboard and Touch Navigation**:
  - Fully compatible for all users.
- **Smooth Scrolling**:
  - Enhances usability and navigation.
	
---

### **Responsive Design**
- **Mobile-Friendly**:
  - Adjusts seamlessly for viewing on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- **Dynamic Styling**:
  - Buttons, text, and layouts adapt to screen dimensions for an optimal user experience.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5**:
  - Provides the structural foundation for content and layout.
- **CSS3**:
  - **Neumorphism-Inspired Styling**: Modern, sleek design for buttons and elements.
  - **Responsive Design**: Media queries for device-friendly scaling.
- **JavaScript**:
  - Dynamic content generation (random images and quotes).
  - Smooth scrolling and toggle functionality.
- **Instagram API**:
  - Integration for embedding posts.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Explore the Page**:
   - Reload the page to discover a new quote and image of Trent.
   - Use the scroll buttons for quick navigation.

2. **View the Gallery**:
   - Click the **Gallery** button to reveal a collection of Trent’s photos.
   - Close the gallery when done to return to the main page.

3. **Connect on Social Media**:
   - Click the **Social Links** button to view embedded Instagram posts about Trent.
   - Follow the links for more stories and adventures.
