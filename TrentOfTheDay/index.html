Trent - Son of Reznor

Trent, the second generation stoner dog, was a misfit rarity of amazing proportions. He looked like an Ewok, and both dogs and people didn't know what to make of him. With a face so ugly that it was cute, he could stop people in their tracks, making them laugh. Trent looked dashing in a bow tie and was deeply loved by his human mother, the lovely Laura, whom he followed everywhere.

He was a beautiful couch potato, always happy to sleep and relax, but he also loved beach runs and adventures with his family across the U.S., including hikes around waterfalls and national forests. Trent lived a full, happy, and love-filled 15 years in various places, including Minneapolis, Portland, and coastal Oregon.
