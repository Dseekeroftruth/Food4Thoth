Introduction

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Rstory, Artabillies, TUDE tokens, and DeJahn's gallery. This page serves as a detailed repository of information, links, and resources about the remarkable efforts and creativity behind these projects.

About Rstory and TUDE Tokens

Rstory is an innovative cryptocurrency token operating on both the Ethereum and EOS blockchains. These tokens, known as "tudes," represent indivisible units of gratitude and grant access to exclusive digital goods, such as science fiction novels by Mark Bailey and other premium content.

Key Features

  • Ethereum Token: TUDE (Contract Address: 0xa9deffb371dd9c7fd2cfc
    6676cdb3bc394b45a42    )
  • EOS : RSTORY (Viewable on Blocks.io)
  • Offers exclusive access to digital goods, including novels and high-resolution digital art.
  • Supports the decentralized distribution of creative content.

Exclusive Content

By holding at least one Rstory token, users unlock access to a collection of seven science fiction novels by Mark Bailey. These novels are available for free as PDF downloads for token holders. For non-token holders, the books are also available for purchase in print via Blurb and for Kindle on Amazon.

Available Novels:

  • The Paradise Anomaly: Available in print and Kindle editions. PDF accessible to token holders.
  • One Man Embassy
  • Flying Saucer Shenanigans
  • Rainbow Lullaby
  • The Ostermann Method
  • Blue Dragon Mississippi
  • Psychic Avalanche

How to Access Content

  1. Acquire Tokens: Purchase TUDE tokens on the Ethereum network or RSTORY tokens on the EOS network via platforms like Alcor Exchange.
  2. Connect Your Wallet: Use MetaMask for Ethereum or Anchor for EOS to connect your wallet to the Rstory platform.
  3. Authenticate and Unlock: After authentication, PDF links for the novels will become active, allowing you to download and enjoy the content.

Roadmap

The Rstory project outlines a clear roadmap for growth and innovation:

  • 2019: Creation on Bitshares and Waves by Insight Green, LLC.
  • 2021: Acquisition by Mark Bailey; introduction of RSTORY on EOS and TUDE on Ethereum.
  • 2024: Launch of the first digital publishing gateway on rstory.io; listing of RSTORY on Alcor and TUDE on Uniswap.
  • 2025: Planned release of the Rstory integration kit to fuel network growth.
  • 2027: Aspiration to become the leading network for alternative media and the arts.

Why Choose Rstory?

  • Affordable Access: Token holders save over $30 compared to purchasing novels individually.
  • Creative Ecosystem: Supports blockchain-based ownership of creative content and strengthens connections between creators and their audience.
  • Community-Driven: Encourages decentralized contributions and fosters an inclusive platform for alternative media and the arts.

About Artabillies

Artabillies is a creative art collective established in 2016 by Laura and DeJahn Bell. Its mission is to provide a platform that fosters diverse, eclectic, and vibrant artistic expressions. Over the years, it has evolved into a supportive community that embraces a broad spectrum of artistic endeavors, including drawings, prints, wearable art, music, and more.

Highlights

  • Grassroots Creativity: Artabillies merges traditional and contemporary art styles, drawing inspiration from "Rockabilly" and "Hillbilly" cultures. It celebrates individuality while honoring tradition and nature.
  • Community Engagement: The collective organizes free, all-ages musical gatherings, live art installations, and hands-on teaching sessions covering painting, sewing, and music. It also fosters networking and collaboration among artists.
  • Environmental Commitment: Adopts eco-friendly materials and practices in art production and shipping. It raises ecological awareness through environmental art projects and conservation initiatives.
  • Future Goals: Artabillies plans to expand its influence through international collaborations, permanent art centers, and art therapy programs that promote mental health and well-being.

Recognition

Artabillies was recently awarded 1 million Rstory Gratitude Tokens (TUDEs) by Rstory, acknowledging its contributions to the arts and community. This recognition highlights the collective's transformative impact and dedication to inclusivity and sustainability.

DeJahn and the "Do You Do Dobbs" Gallery

DeJahn is a celebrated artist whose work lies at the intersection of psychedelic aesthetics, mixed media, and cutting-edge digital innovation. His "Do You Do Dobbs" gallery is a whimsical and vibrant display of his talent, blending elegance with playful absurdity.

Art Description

The gallery features a sophisticated gentleman adorned with a classic top hat and monocle, engaging in an unexpected activity: cannabis dabs. This distinguished figure is surrounded by a psychedelic aura of vibrant rainbow hues, creating a surreal visual effect. Adding a humorous twist, a speech bubble asks, "Do you do Dobbs?", juxtaposing aristocratic sophistication with playful, laid-back creativity. This delightful contrast captures DeJahn's unique style, blending tradition with bold, imaginative expression.

About DeJahn

  • Renowned for his psychedelic aesthetics, intricate patterns, and surreal imagery.
  • Explores themes of nature, the cosmos, and the human mind through his art.
  • Creates immersive works that captivate viewers and invite introspection.

Tokenized Access to DeJahn’s Gallery

Access to DeJahn’s high-resolution artwork is token-gated, requiring either RSTORY or TUDE tokens. These tokens serve as digital keys, unlocking premium content such as high-quality PNG or JPEG downloads and exclusive event access.

Token Details

  • RSTORY Token: Available on the EOS network and accessible via the Anchor Wallet. RSTORY supports creative collaborations in the digital art world.
  • TUDE Token: Functions on the Ethereum network and can be accessed through the MetaMask Wallet. TUDE connects a global audience to digital art offerings.

How to Access the Gallery

  1. Acquire Tokens: Purchase RSTORY or TUDE tokens on cryptocurrency markets such as Alcor Exchange.
  2. Verify Your Wallet: Connect your wallet (Anchor for EOS or MetaMask for Ethereum) to the gallery for token verification.
  3. Download Art: Unlock and download high-resolution versions of DeJahn’s artwork in PNG or JPEG formats.

The Future of Art with RSTORY

  • Innovative Platform: RSTORY merges blockchain technology with creative expression to support next-generation digital artists.
  • Community and Vision: Token holders become part of a network where art, technology, and community converge.
  • Collaborative Ecosystem: Encourages community participation and fosters decentralized ownership of digital art.

Conclusion

DeJahn’s "Do You Do Dobbs" gallery represents the future of digital art, combining exclusivity with accessibility through tokenized ownership. By holding an RSTORY or TUDE token, you can unlock premium content and become part of a groundbreaking movement that redefines the relationship between art and technology. Explore, engage, and experience the world of DeJahn like never before.
