# 🌟 What Day Were You Born?
_A Mystical Cosmic Profile Generator_

---

## 📖 Overview

**What Day Were You Born?** is an interactive cosmic tool that reveals deep insights based on your birthdate.  
Using astrology, numerology, moon phases, the Chinese Zodiac, and real historical data, it builds a **personalized Cosmic Profile** — offering reflections on your energy, destiny, and cosmic story.

This tool automatically analyzes your birthdate and displays:
- Day of the week  
- Zodiac sign, planetary rulers, elements, and modality  
- Chinese Zodiac animal and elemental profile  
- Moon phase & emotional rhythm  
- Life path number  
- Important historical events and famous births  
- A custom-generated AstroPoem and Life Suggestions  

Beautifully designed with **dark mode support**, **neumorphic navigation**, **collapsible sections**, and **downloadable summaries**.

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. Enter your birthdate.
2. Click **Find Day**.
3. Instantly see:
   - Your day of birth
   - Astrological zodiac traits
   - Chinese zodiac energy and elemental description
   - Your Moon phase and emotional tendencies
   - Life path number and destiny
   - Downloadable full Cosmic Profile
4. Toggle **Dark Mode** for a night-view aesthetic.
5. **Copy Share Link** to share your results!

## 🎨 Technologies Used

- **HTML5**
- **CSS3** (Advanced styling, gradients, neumorphism, dark mode)
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **SunCalc.js** (moon illumination and moon phase calculation)
- **Wikipedia REST API** (for historical events and famous birthdays)
- **Custom Cosmic Data Mappings** (Zodiac, Chinese Elements, Moon Phases)

---

## ⚡ Highlighted Code Techniques

- **Preloading critical assets** (`fonts`, `images`, `scripts`)
- **Graceful fallback for SunCalc if CDN fails**
- **Dynamic content generation** via `innerHTML` and collapsible containers
- **Local storage** to save Dark Mode preference
- **Clipboard API** to copy Share Links
- **Blob API** to **download personalized Cosmic Profile**
- **Animated collapsible sections** for better user experience
- **Mobile-friendly scaling and responsiveness**
