The Brockton Exodus

On the planet Mother, the average lifespan is 120 years. This story begins on Mother's Temperate Subcontinent, in the city of New Oro, on the campus of the archivists. There, while Motherling archivists observe Earthlings across the galaxy using special technology, something happens that threatens to upend the balance of power on their distant utopian world.

Small Gods of Time Travel

A man travels back in time to the day before 9/11. Once there, he meets up with his younger self. Together, using information and technology from the future, they try to improve society. Their most ambitious goal is changing the outcome of the US 2016 Democratic Primary. Along the way, powerful future technology falls into the wrong hands, with unexpected results.

The 321 Incident

A national tragedy claims as many lives as 9/11 did. From there, the story follows a handful of characters as they try to navigate the post-tragedy landscape. Although they face steep challenges, synchronicities and the magic of human connections help them find their way. Politics, media, harm reduction, and non-ordinary states of consciousness are some of the themes that the work explores.

The Paradise Anomaly

It begins in Brooklyn. The Fairie Queen sends a pair of hipsters on a grand quest that ends in another world. In this other world, the guardian of the passage to Earth faces hard choices after learning troubling information about his predecessor. Then a billionaire's pet project upends this world's traditional society. The pitfalls of hidden politics are uncovered through dark humor and casual intrigue.

One Man Embassy

Forced to flee his home planet Jhanya during a violent revolution, Hequa steals a spaceship and makes his way to Earth. Here, on an unfamiliar world with alien customs, he tries to make a life for himself. Aided by technology from Jhanya and hindered by the absence of identity documents, Hequa finds work as a panhandler, a stage magician, and a manufacturer of rare items. Eventually, he opens Jhanya Embassy, where he shares the wonders of his home planet with the people of Earth.

Flying Saucer Shenanigans

On the planet Jhanya, the destruction of a space station is blamed on a revolutionary named Hequa. When Hequa flees Jhanya for Earth, Lika the assassin is sent after him. On Earth, Lika pursues her target while learning to navigate the new planet's strange customs. She eventually makes some friends, finds a job washing dishes, and comes to realize that she may have been lied to by her government.

Rainbow Lullaby

A Lagu is an ancient and magical medium-sized mammal. When one of these strange creatures is accidentally seen by a human boy named Luke, everything changes. Traha is a sentient tree from a planet 23 light years away. When her one-way journey to Earth is completed with the help of a sorcerer named Taavi, everything changes. The blue man is many things. A safe driver. An inspiration. A lover of root beer floats. When the blue man surprises a woman named Nneka at a shopping mall, everything changes. These intertwined tales unfold in circumstances where magical creatures, telepathy, a revolutionary new currency, and a mysteriously powerful dance come together in unlikely ways. Through it all, the everyday world is transformed into someplace unfamiliar, and the reader is left questioning everything but the value of friendship.

The Ostermann Method

A former spy tells the story of his clandestine career. A network of sorcerers attempt to influence the stock market using hypnosis. An act of terrorism provokes unsettling questions. And the murder of a bartender in Chicago threatens to transform a secret war into something entirely unexpected.

Blue Dragon Mississippi

Pikwik the blue dragon has lived in the Mississippi River for thousands of years. Like all blue dragons, Pikwik's life is enriched by her psychic communications with the people in her territory. When Pikwik realizes that fascists are beginning to take over, she chooses a young woman named Matilda to champion an anti-fascist resistance movement. With Pikwik's help, Matilda finds support for this movement in an ex-magician, an alien from another planet, and various other unusual characters. They might not have much in common, but they all agree that fascism sucks. And sometimes that's enough.

Psychic Avalanche

An English wizard and an extraterrestrial combine magic with science to create a new drug that makes people telepathic. This has immediate consequences for a crypto enthusiast and his friends. It also reshapes society in unexpected ways. Fifty years in the future, an adventurous young woman embarks on a quest to uncover the secrets of the mysterious wizard who started it all.

Distribution: Rstory tokens are given away to people who contribute to the project. They are also used for virtual philanthropy, given to people and organizations working in service to the public good. Beyond this, the Rstory token is sold on the open market, where it may be purchased by anyone.

Process: Adding value to the network qualifies you to receive Rstory tokens. Just add your own book or video or song or other digital good to the pool of awesome content available exclusively to Rstory token holders and we'll send you a quantity of tokens proportional to your contribution. Here's the basic process:

  1. Digital good or webpage is uploaded to IPFS
  2. The address of the file is encrypted
  3. The file location is revealed only to people who possess Rstory
  4. The first ones to share their work in this way will be showcased here on rstory.io, though anyone is welcome to make their own site or app that integrates the token
  5. As more people contribute to the content pool, everyone's work is shared with a wider and wider audience

Connect: If you want to get involved or just want to learn more, the easiest way to connect is on Telegram

Rstory is infrastructure for the emerging decentralized economy, powered by a crypto token with zero decimals that operates on multiple blockchains. Rstory tokens are called tudes. Each one represents an indivisible unit of gratitude. Possession of a tude on any chain grants the bearer the right to access electronic copies of Mark Bailey's science fiction novels.

There are 10 awesome novels available on this page. To read them, just connect your Anchor wallet, MetaMask wallet (browser extension only, mobile app support coming soon), or DropChain account. If there's at least one Rstory token in your wallet, the book links will become active.

This project is also set up to make it easy for anyone to participate by making their own premium digital goods exclusively accessible to token holders. Doing this qualifies you to receive a supply of Rstory tokens that can be sold, traded, or given away.

Hi, I'm Mark Bailey. My science fiction novels form the initial basis for the value of the Rstory token. But Rstory wasn't created just to distribute my books. It was created to make it easy for anyone to publish their own digital goods in a decentralized and censorship-resistant way.

If you have great content you'd like to share exclusively with the Rstory network, I'll help you integrate Rstory into your site and freely give you a quantity of tokens that you can sell on the open market or give away to your audience.

My books are available below. I spent 5 years producing these 10 volumes. If you have a token, I recommend starting with One Man Embassy. If you don't yet have a token, you can check out The Brockton Exodus or Small Gods of Time Travel for free. For a better sense of this collection and what it's about, watch an 18 min interview about my work.

The novels showcased here are short and funny, with dialog driving the plot forward. They explore emerging technologies, non-ordinary states of consciousness, and modern mythologies. In the process, these stories dig deep into some of society's most pressing problems and occasionally come up with unexpected insights regarding solutions.

Along with writing novels, I publish Free Mind Gazette on Substack and maintain a Hive blog. I'm based in Minneapolis and have worked as a news editor for WantToKnow.info since 2014.
