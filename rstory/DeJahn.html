DeJahn: Mystic Technologist, Creative Architect, and Cultural Alchemist

I'm DeJahn, a multidisciplinary artist, psychedelic systems thinker, the creative force behind Food4Thoth.com and co-creator of Artabillies.com — platforms evolving to amplify imagination, decentralize power, and catalyze artistic liberation.

My work flows between worlds — from hand-cut collage and UV-reactive mixed media to immersive web comics, fractal tarot tools, music collections, and AI-enhanced experimental design. At the heart of everything I create is a mission to re-enchant the world through art, mysticism, and mutual aid.

Artabillies: Art for the People, by the People

Founded in 2016, Artabillies is a grassroots art collective and gallery built in collaboration with my wife Laura Bella. What began as a DIY vending project evolved into a national platform featuring vibrant, eclectic works from artists I deeply respect — people like Izwel, Chuck Hues, Rob Munger, DoodleTek, Mary Thompson, Northern Groove, and many more.

Artabillies showcases:

  • Psychedelic and outsider art
  • Wearable creations, prints, glasswork, and illustrations
  • A reverence for both tradition and experimentation
  • Eco-conscious shipping, accessible pricing, and direct artist support

“Don’t feed the artist — buy their art and they’ll feed themselves.”

Distribution: Rstory tokens are given away to people who contribute to the project. They are also used for virtual philanthropy, given to people and organizations working in service to the public good. Beyond this, the Rstory token is sold on the open market, where it may be purchased by anyone.

Process: Adding value to the network qualifies you to receive Rstory tokens. Just add your own book or video or song or other media product to the pool of awesome content available exclusively to Rstory token holders and we'll send you a quantity of tokens proportional to your contribution. Here's the basic process:

  1. Media or webpage is uploaded to IPFS
  2. The address of the file is encrypted
  3. The file location is revealed only to people who possess Rstory
  4. The first ones to share their work in this way will be showcased here on rstory.io, though anyone is welcome to make their own site or app that integrates the token
  5. As more people contribute to the content pool, everyone's work is shared with a wider and wider audience
