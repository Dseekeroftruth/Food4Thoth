About DeJahn

DeJahn has been cultivating an interdisciplinary creative hub — where digital code meets intuitive mysticism. With deep roots in collaborative storytelling and a bold artistic voice, he channels collective expression through works like Food4Thoth, a psychedelic archive and interactive playground of esoteric tools, visual art, and spiritual inquiry.

Through Artabillies, DeJahn uplifts independent artists with a mission to fuse traditional and digital mediums. In 2024, Artabillies received 1 million RSTORY gratitude tokens — highlighting its impact on the creative economy.
